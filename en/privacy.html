Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-16

Zepunari is intentionally simple: no account, no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no automatic collection of personal data during gameplay.

  • The app does not automatically collect, upload, or share personal information from children or parents during gameplay.
  • No account is required. The app does not ask for names, dates of birth, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details.
  • The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or precise location.
  • The current version contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not use IDFA.
  • Core gameplay, settings, and built-in music run locally on the device. There are no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

Support email

If a parent chooses to contact support by email, the email service processes the sender's address and the message content to deliver the message and enable a reply. Please do not include a child's name, photos, location, school, or other unnecessary personal information. This is separate from the offline core gameplay.

Website hosting and IP address logs

Our privacy and support webpages are hosted on GitHub Pages. According to GitHub's official documentation, GitHub records and stores visitors' IP addresses for security purposes when a GitHub Pages website is visited, whether or not the visitor is signed in. Visiting these pages is separate from Zepunari's offline core gameplay. GitHub Pages documentation · GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email correspondence, retention and privacy rights

Messages are sent only when someone chooses to send them using their own email application. Google Gmail and the person handling Zepunari support may process the sender's address, subject, message and voluntarily attached files to answer the request and provide directly related follow-up. Please do not send unnecessary personal information about children. Support correspondence is kept only while necessary for the request and related follow-up; when a case ends, the need for further retention is reviewed. Specific legal, security or dispute obligations may require longer retention. We do not promise automatic permanent erasure within a fixed number of days.

For access to, correction or deletion of your own support correspondence, contact fivechaoice@gmail.com. We may verify that a request concerns your own messages. Where the EU GDPR applies, the legal basis for necessary support replies is legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)); legally required privacy-rights handling may rely on legal obligation (Article 6(1)(c)). Depending on applicable law, rights may include access, correction, erasure, restriction and objection, and lodging a complaint with a data protection authority. Gmail may process email in different countries; consult Google's privacy and data-transfer notices. These optional communications and GitHub Pages visits are separate from offline gameplay.
