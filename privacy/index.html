隱私權政策

最後更新：2026 年 9 月 21 日

Zepunari 是一款為 5 歲及以下幼兒設計的離線旅行遊戲。我們把產品做得簡單：不要求帳號、不放廣告、不加入第三方追蹤，也不在遊戲過程中自動收集個人資料。

1. App 內資料收集

  • 遊戲過程中不會自動收集、上傳或分享兒童或家長的個人資料。
  • 不需要建立帳號，也不要求姓名、生日、Email、電話或其他聯絡資料。
  • 不要求相機、麥克風、照片、通訊錄或精確位置權限。
  • 目前版本不包含第三方分析、廣告或追蹤 SDK，也不使用 IDFA。

2. 離線運作

核心遊戲、設定與內建音樂都在裝置本地運作，不需要我們的遊戲後端服務。Zepunari 目前沒有 App 內購買或訂閱。

3. 支援 Email

只有當家長主動寄信給支援信箱時，Email 服務才會處理寄件地址及信件中自願提供的內容，目的僅為回覆支援。請不要寄送兒童姓名、照片、位置、學校或其他不必要的個人資料。

Zepunari · Made for ages 5 and under

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Zepunari is an offline travel game made for children ages 5 and under. The product is intentionally simple: no account, no advertising, no third-party tracking, and no automatic collection of personal data during gameplay.

1. Data collection in the app

  • The app does not automatically collect, upload, or share personal information from children or parents during gameplay.
  • No account is required. The app does not ask for names, dates of birth, email addresses, phone numbers, or other contact details.
  • The app does not request access to the camera, microphone, photos, contacts, or precise location.
  • The current version contains no third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs and does not use IDFA.

2. Offline operation

Core gameplay, settings, and built-in music run locally on the device and do not require a Zepunari gameplay backend. The current version has no in-app purchases or subscriptions.

3. Support email

If a parent voluntarily emails support, the email service processes the sender address and any information the sender chooses to include so that we can reply. Please do not send a child's name, photo, location, school, or other unnecessary personal information.
